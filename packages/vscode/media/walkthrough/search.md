## Search and Navigate

CommandVault provides three search tiers:

1. **Fuse.js** — Fuzzy matching with typo tolerance
2. **MiniSearch** — Indexed search with prefix matching
3. **SQLite FTS5** — Full-text search with boolean queries

Use `Cmd+Shift+V` (Mac) or `Ctrl+Shift+V` to open the search palette. Results are ranked by relevance, usage frequency, and recency.
