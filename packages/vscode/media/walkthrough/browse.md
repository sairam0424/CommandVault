## Browse Your Commands

CommandVault indexes all AI commands from your `~/.claude/` directory:

- **Skills** — Reusable capabilities invoked with `/skill-name`
- **Agents** — Specialized AI personas for specific tasks
- **Commands** — Custom slash commands organized by namespace
- **Plugins** — Installed Claude Code plugins
- **Rules** — Global behavior rules and coding standards
- **Hooks** — Pre/post tool-use automation hooks

Click the CommandVault icon in the activity bar to open the sidebar.
